# Carapace

[🇷🇺 Русский](carapace.ru.md)

A multi-shell completion engine with bridges to other shells' completions.

## Paths

- **In the repo:** `tui/carapace`
- **Linked to:** `~/.config/carapace`

## What differs from the defaults

- `bridges.yaml` takes completions from `fish` for tools that may lack native completions elsewhere: `atuin`, `brew`,
  `bun`, `caffeinate`, `dust`, `ffmpeg`, `ffprobe`, `jq`, `kill`, `lsof`, `mpc`, `pip`, `pip3`, `tmux`, `uv`, `zellij`.
