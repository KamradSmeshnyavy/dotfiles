# Jolt

[🇷🇺 Русский](jolt.ru.md)

A power and resource monitor with history.

## Paths

- **In the repo:** `tui/jolt`
- **Linked to:** `~/.config/jolt`

## What differs from the defaults

- **UI:** dark appearance, graphs on with merged metrics, refresh every 2000 ms, top 50 processes.
- **Monitoring:** power threshold 0.5 W, forecast window 300 s.
- **Units:** energy in Wh, temperature in °C, data in SI.
- **History:** raw data 30 days, hourly 180 days, sessions 90 days; database up to 500 MB.
